Frequently asked questions

How to order from Moplaco.

Samples, minimum order quantities, pricing, grading, and shipping — everything a first-time buyer needs to know before reaching out.

i

Before you read on: Moplaco is a green coffee exporter, not a sourcing agency. We buy parchment and red cherry directly from farmers and our own washing stations across seven Ethiopian origins — plus our own forest farm in Sheka — and then dry-mill, clean, grade, and export that coffee ourselves. What you see on Our Coffees is what we already hold or are actively milling this harvest, not a menu of lots we'll go source for you on request.

About Moplaco
Is Moplaco a coffee sourcing agency that finds custom lots on request?+
No. Moplaco is a green coffee processor and exporter — we don't go and source a bespoke lot to a buyer's specification. We buy cherry and parchment from farmers and our own washing stations, dry-mill and grade it ourselves, and sell what that harvest actually produces. If a lot isn't in stock or in the current milling run, we can't commission it into existence.
Do you grow all the coffee yourselves?+
Only a small share of it. Our Sheka site is Moplaco's own forest farm. Everywhere else — Yirgacheffe, Sidamo, Guji, Harrar, Limu, and Jimma — we buy cherry or parchment from smallholder farmers and our own washing stations, then handle the dry-milling, grading, and export ourselves. We've operated this way since 1971.
Samples & ordering
How do I try Moplaco coffee before committing to a container?+
Request a free green coffee sample through Shop Samples. Pick a lot, tell us where to send it, and submit — no payment required upfront. Samples usually ship within a week; you (or your freight forwarder) cover the courier cost.
What's the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?+
Standard export MOQ is one full container — roughly 19.2 MT, or 320 bags at 60kg — per origin and lot. Smaller or consolidated orders can sometimes be arranged for repeat buyers; ask us directly via Contact Us.
Can I request a custom blend, a specific grade, or a certified lot?+
Within reason. Because we sell from what we've already bought and milled this harvest, availability is bounded by what a given station or farm actually produced — we can quote against current stock and processing plans, but we can't commission a bespoke lot from scratch. Certifications such as Organic or Fair Trade apply only to specific origins and lots (see each profile on Our Coffees) — we can't retroactively certify uncertified stock.
Pricing & payment
How is pricing quoted?+
FOB Djibouti (or another agreed port), per shipment, in USD. Pricing moves with the harvest calendar and the export market, so we quote current terms directly — send your target origin, grade, and volume through Contact Us and our Addis Ababa team will respond within 48 hours.
What payment terms do you accept?+
Telegraphic Transfer (T/T) with a deposit on order confirmation and the balance against shipping documents is our standard term. Letters of Credit (L/C) are accepted for established buyers on larger volumes.
Quality & documentation
How is the coffee cleaned, graded, and prepared for export?+
Cherry or parchment arriving at our stations is dry-milled — hulled, sorted by density and screen size, and hand- or optically-sorted to remove defects — then graded to Ethiopian export standards (Grade 1 through 5, by defect count and bean size) and packed in GrainPro or vacuum liners inside jute outer bags for the voyage.
What documentation comes with a shipment?+
A certificate of origin, phytosanitary certificate, quality/grading certificate, and bill of lading as standard. For EU-bound shipments we also provide our EUDR due-diligence documentation — see EUDR Compliance for details.
Shipping & getting started
How long does it take from order to arrival?+
Confirmed orders typically leave our warehouse within 2–4 weeks, depending on current stock and the harvest season. Ocean transit time on top of that depends entirely on your destination port — ask us for an estimate to your location.
How do I actually place an order?+
Use the Contact Us form and tell us the origin, process, grade, and volume you're after (for example: "1 container of Yirgacheffe washed, grade 1"). Our team in Addis Ababa responds within 48 hours.
